XClip B-Trip Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

California Suncap LLC · Updated November 4, 2026

Scope and contact

B-Trip is operated by California Suncap LLC. This policy describes the current app. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy questions, contact privacy@suncapca.com. It does not replace your employer’s policies for company records.

适用范围与联系

B-Trip 由 California Suncap LLC 运营。本政策说明当前 App 的数据处理。访问、更正、删除或其他隐私问题请联系 privacy@suncapca.com。公司记录也可能受你所在公司的管理政策约束。

Data you provide

Depending on the features you use, we handle your account email and identity; company and membership details; issues and dated updates; receipts, merchants, dates, amounts and tax fields; business-card contacts and notes; photos, PDFs and imported content; reports and company membership. Existing records from previous versions may also include audio or transcripts.

你提供的数据

根据你使用的功能，我们处理账号邮箱与身份、公司及成员资料、事项与带时间的跟进、票据商家/日期/金额/税额、名片联系人与备注、照片/PDF/导入内容、报告与公司成员身份。旧版本留下的记录也可能含音频或转写文字。

Location and device access

Location-related features can process precise location, timestamps, direction, movement and recorded route points. Access depends on iOS permissions and the features actually enabled; do not assume every trip is being recorded. Camera, selected-photo and file access support capture/import. You can change permissions in iOS Settings; related features may stop working.

定位及设备权限

定位相关功能可能处理精确位置、时间、方向、移动状态及路线坐标。访问取决于 iOS 授权及实际启用的功能，不代表每趟行程都在记录。相机、所选照片和文件访问用于拍摄及导入。你可在 iOS 设置修改权限，对应功能可能因此不可用。

Why data is used

We use data to save and retrieve your records, extract and verify document fields, organize travel and expense reports, authenticate accounts, enforce company membership permissions, and respond to errors or support requests. Previously saved historical records remain retained.

处理目的

数据用于保存与查看记录、提取和核对单据字段、整理行程与支出报告、登录认证、校验公司成员权限，以及处理故障或支持请求。旧版本的历史资料仍予保留。

AI document processing

Using receipt or business-card extraction sends OCR text and necessary request context through our server-side service to Alibaba Cloud’s AI service. Text may include names, contact information and financial details visible in the source. The current extraction request contains OCR text, not the original image. Originals or PDFs may separately be uploaded or included in reports when those features are used. Do not send information you are not entitled to process.

AI 单据解析

使用收据或名片解析时，OCR 文字与必要请求上下文会经服务端发送至阿里云 AI 服务。文字可能含原件上的姓名、联系方式及财务资料。当前解析请求发送 OCR 文字，不附原始图片；使用上传或报告功能时，原图/PDF 可能另外被上传或加入报告。请勿提交无权处理的信息。

Cloud services and international processing

Supabase supports account authentication, selected cloud files, company data. Alibaba Cloud supports AI extraction; the current AI endpoint is in Singapore. Processing and service operations can occur outside your home country. Service providers process relevant data under their applicable terms; this app does not promise zero retention by providers. Importing a receipt link requests the supplied public URL; the remote website receives ordinary request information such as your IP address and requested URL. Operational services may retain technical request or error metadata. Apple system services may also process data when you use system features such as Maps, iCloud files or the share sheet.

云端服务与跨地区处理

Supabase 支持账号认证、相关云端文件、公司资料；阿里云提供 AI 解析，当前 AI 接口位于新加坡。数据处理及服务运维可能发生在你所在国家或地区之外。服务商依据其适用条款处理相关资料，本 App 不承诺服务商零保留。导入网络单据链接时会请求你提供的公开网址，远端网站会接收到 IP 地址及所请求网址等普通请求信息。服务运维也可能保留请求或错误等技术元数据。使用地图、iCloud 文件、系统分享等功能时，Apple 系统服务也可能处理相关数据。

Login email and Apple subscriptions

Login codes are delivered through Resend using your email address. Supabase supports B-Trip account and subscription records. Apple processes subscription purchases; B-Trip receives signed transaction identifiers, product and renewal information, and uses an account token to link the subscription to the purchasing account. B-Trip does not receive your payment card details from Apple. TestFlight purchases use Apple Sandbox.

登录邮件与 Apple 订阅

登录验证码通过 Resend 发送至你的邮箱。Supabase 支持 B-Trip 账号和订阅记录。Apple 处理订阅购买；B-Trip 接收签名交易编号、商品及续订信息，并以账号令牌关联购买账号。B-Trip 不会从 Apple 接收你的银行卡资料。TestFlight 购买使用 Apple 沙盒。

Personal, company and shared data

Personal and Company spaces use distinct record scopes. Personal does not mean offline: AI extraction, uploads and sharing can still transmit relevant content. Company access is controlled by account membership. Share actions can send full PDFs, receipts, names and route information to the recipient you choose. Your employer or the recipient may retain their copy.

个人、公司与分享范围

个人与公司空间使用不同的记录范围。个人空间不等于离线：AI 解析、上传和分享仍可能发送相关内容。公司资料访问受账号成员权限控制。分享可能将完整 PDF、票据、姓名和路线发给你选择的接收方，所在公司或接收方可能保留其副本。

Storage, retention and deletion

Records and originals may be stored on the device; uploaded files, company sync data and approval records may also be stored in the cloud. Some replacements retain previous originals or versions. Retention depends on the purpose, account status, audit requirements and applicable obligations; no single deletion period applies to all records. Signing out or uninstalling does not erase cloud copies or other recipients’ copies. Use available record controls for local records, and contact us for account/cloud access or deletion requests. Company audit records or backups may require separate handling.

保存、保留与删除

记录及原件可能保存在设备上；已上传文件、公司同步资料和审批记录也可能保存在云端。部分替换操作保留旧原件或版本。保留时间取决于用途、账号状态、审计需求及适用义务，不对所有资料设定同一删除期限。退出登录或卸载不会抹除云端及接收方副本。本机记录使用页面提供的管理操作；账号或云端访问、删除请求请联系我们。公司审计记录和备份可能需要单独处理。

Security and your choices

The app uses account authentication, server-side authorization, transport security and protected local storage where implemented. No system is guaranteed perfectly secure. Limit sensitive content in documents and reports; verify sharing destinations. You can decline device permissions and avoid upload, AI extraction or sharing features, although some workflows will then be unavailable.

安全与选择

App 使用账号认证、服务端权限校验、传输保护及已实现的本机存储保护，但任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。请减少单据与报告中的不必要敏感内容，并核对分享对象。你可拒绝设备权限，也可不使用上传、AI 解析和分享功能，对应流程可能因此不可用。

Advertising, requests and updates

The current app does not include an advertising-tracking feature. For privacy requests, email privacy@suncapca.com with the account and scope involved; do not send passwords, access tokens or API keys. We may need to verify identity and coordinate with your company for company records. Available rights and exceptions depend on applicable law. Material changes to this policy will be reflected in its updated date.

广告、隐私请求与更新

当前 App 不包含广告跟踪功能。提出隐私请求请向 privacy@suncapca.com 说明涉及的账号及范围，不要发送密码、访问令牌或 API 密钥。我们可能需要核验身份，公司记录可能需与你所在公司协调。可行使的权利及例外以适用法律为准。政策有重要变化时会更新日期。
